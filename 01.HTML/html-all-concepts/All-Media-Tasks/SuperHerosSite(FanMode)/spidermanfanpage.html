SPIDER-MAN

Spider-Man Music Over the Years 1960s TV cartoon: Famous theme song “Spider-Man, Spider-Man, does whatever a spider can…” became iconic. Sam Raimi Trilogy (2002–2007): Danny Elfman composed dramatic, emotional orchestral scores. Amazing Spider-Man films (2012–2014): James Horner & Hans Zimmer made more modern, darker, electronic styles. Spider-Man in MCU (2016–present): Michael Giacchino composed the soundtrack. He even remixed the 1960s cartoon theme for fun in Homecoming. Spider-Verse animated films: They use a mix of hip-hop, modern pop, and electronic beats, which makes them super popular with younger fans. So Spider-Man’s music changes with each era, but always tries to match his youthful, energetic character.

SPIDER-MAN

About Spider-Man Real name: Peter Parker. Powers: After being bitten by a radioactive spider, he gains super strength, agility, wall-crawling, and a “spider-sense” that warns him of danger. Personality: A young, relatable hero—smart, funny, sometimes awkward, but always brave. Why people love him: He’s not a god (like Thor) or billionaire (like Iron Man); he’s just a normal teenager trying to balance life, school, and hero work. That makes him very relatable and one of Marvel’s most popular characters worldwide.

Avengers

Who are the Avengers? The Avengers are a team of superheroes in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) and also in Marvel Comics. Their mission: protect the Earth (and sometimes the universe) from powerful threats that no single hero can face alone. They are often called “Earth’s Mightiest Heroes.”
