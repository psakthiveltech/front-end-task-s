Thor

Thor is a superhero in the Avengers movies from Marvel. He is the God of Thunder, originally from Asgard (a different realm). He has a powerful weapon called Mjolnir (a magical hammer), later also Stormbreaker (a huge axe). His powers include superhuman strength, speed, durability, the ability to summon lightning, and flight (when using his weapon). His personality: At first, he was arrogant and proud, but later he becomes wiser, brave, and protective of Earth and his friends. Actor Chris Hemsworth plays Thor in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU). In the Avengers team, Thor often provides the raw power and godly strength needed to fight enemies like Loki, Ultron, and Thanos.

Thor

The soundtrack of Thor: Ragnarok (2017, directed by Taika Waititi) is one of the most unique in all Marvel movies. Here’s the breakdown: 🎼 Main Composer Mark Mothersbaugh (from the band Devo) created the original score. His music mixes synthwave / retro 80s electronic sounds with orchestral superhero themes, giving the movie a colorful, fun, and cosmic vibe.

Avengers

Who are the Avengers? The Avengers are a team of superheroes in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) and also in Marvel Comics. Their mission: protect the Earth (and sometimes the universe) from powerful threats that no single hero can face alone. They are often called “Earth’s Mightiest Heroes.”
